Maker

Made slowly, from sketch to silver.

Our handmade process moves from hand sketching to 3D modeling, casting, assembling, and polishing. The studio keeps the pace human so every piece can hold the care that made it.

Our difference

Sustainability as a love language.

We believe luxury is knowing where your jewelry comes from. Fine metals let us rework damaged pieces and returns into something new, while biodegradable FSC-certified packaging keeps the care going beyond the studio.

Our process

Four steps, one careful piece.

  1. 01Hand sketching

    Ideas begin on paper, where proportion and softness are worked out before metal is touched.

  2. 023D modeling

    Forms are refined digitally so scale, comfort, and detail can be tested with precision.

  3. 03Mold and casting

    Fine metals are cast with care, keeping material use intentional and waste low.

  4. 04Assembling and polishing

    Each piece is finished by hand, polished slowly, and checked before it leaves the studio.
